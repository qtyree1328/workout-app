Figures & Art demo

window.Figures — animated skeletal SVG figures for climbing exercises. window.Art — deterministic generative topographic cover art. Both are dependency-free classic scripts (no build step).

Phase: work

Climbing figures — light

All 16 exercise ids, mounted and animated on tinted tiles.

Climbing figures — dark container

Same figures; the tile sets --fig-ink (and friends) to light values.

Cover art

Art.cover(seed, category) — a few seeds per category, plus an unrecognised category to show the fallback slate gradient.
